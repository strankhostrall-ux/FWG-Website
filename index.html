ABOUT FWG CLOTHING

LESS NOISE.
MORE PRESENCE.

FWG Clothing is a South African premium streetwear brand built around clean design, strong details and everyday confidence. The brand focuses on essential pieces that are easy to wear and designed to make a clear statement without unnecessary noise.

FWG Clothing was created by Josiah Magoete and Thabiso Lemao. The collection begins with premium T-shirts and is growing into hoodies, jackets and headwear. The FWG approach is simple: refined essentials, confident styling and the mindset to Move With Purpose.
