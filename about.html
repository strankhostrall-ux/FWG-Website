ABOUT FWG CLOTHING

South African Premium Streetwear

FWG Clothing is a South African premium streetwear brand focused on clean design, strong details and everyday confidence. The brand begins with essential T-shirts and is growing into a wider clothing collection.

Our Founders

FWG Clothing was created by Josiah Magoete and Thabiso Lemao. Their brand direction centers on refined everyday clothing and a clear, confident visual identity.

The FWG Approach

FWG is built around the idea of Move With Purpose. The collection focuses on simple silhouettes, premium streetwear styling and details that keep the FWG identity recognizable.

Current

FWG premium T-shirts designed as core everyday streetwear essentials.

Coming Next

Hoodies, jackets and headwear as the FWG clothing collection expands.
